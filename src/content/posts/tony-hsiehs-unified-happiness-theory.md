---
title: "Tony Hsieh's Unified Happiness Theory"
date: "2009-05-19"
---

Tony Hsieh Unified Happiness Theory is not entirely settled. It involves establishing balance among four basic human needs:

1.  perceived progress,
2.  perceived control,
3.  relatedness,
4.  and a connection to a larger vision.

Source: [Zappos Management Style](http://www.inc.com/magazine/20090501/the-zappos-way-of-managing_Printer_Friendly.html)
